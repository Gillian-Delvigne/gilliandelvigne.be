## The need

Modernise the site's visual identity and make it genuinely usable by students and
staff alike. The client had to be able to run their own content after delivery.

## What I did

Wireframes in Adobe XD, then theme development. The choice of WordPress came from
an explicit constraint: editorial autonomy for the client once I was gone.
Carried from design to deployment, in continuous dialogue with stakeholders.

## What I took from it

The real deliverable of an institutional site isn't the site: it's the team's
ability to keep it alive without me. A feature the client doesn't dare use has
not been delivered.
