## The need

The ULB Faculty of Pharmacy wanted to prepare its students for internships and
for structured clinical examinations (OSCE). The existing seminars gave no way to
rehearse a counter situation individually.

## What I did

The instructional design of the simulation series, with academic experts and
practising pharmacists, so that each scenario matched a real situation and
targeted a precise learning outcome. Then the full implementation in VTS Editor:
branching logic, interface, illustrations, interaction flow.

## What I took from it

In a simulation, the hard part isn't writing the right answer — it's writing the
wrong ones. A believable mistake says more about your grasp of a domain than the
expected solution does.
