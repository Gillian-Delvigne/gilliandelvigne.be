## The need

Since November 2018, anyone living in Brussels who applies for a driving licence
must complete four hours of first-aid training. A public contract awarded to the
Belgian Red Cross by Bruxelles Mobilité.

## What I did

The full instructional design of the course, plus a contribution to the
registration platform and the e-learning infrastructure. A blended programme:
asynchronous online modules, then a face-to-face session.

## What I took from it

A mandatory programme cannot afford a vague sign-up flow: every point of friction
turns into a phone call. Interface quality became a line in the budget, which is
the most direct way of proving it matters.
