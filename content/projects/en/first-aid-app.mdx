## The need

Widen public access to first-aid knowledge at low cost, as part of the
organisation's digital transformation.

## The work

Led the development and launch, starting from the open-source codebase provided
by the International Federation (GDPC), adapted to the Belgian context and
aligned with Belgian Red Cross training standards.

The app now has more than **25,000 users** and doubles as a digital companion during
courses, replacing the printed booklet.

## The takeaway

Reusing an existing codebase rather than starting over was the right budget call,
and the least comfortable one: most of the work went into understanding code I
hadn't written before earning the right to touch it.
