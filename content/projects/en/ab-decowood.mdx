## The need

A bespoke joinery and decoration business with no online presence, whose
craftsmanship was nowhere to be seen.

## What I did

Everything: graphic work, an identity aligned with the workshop's output, theme
development, search optimisation and release. WordPress again, so the client kept
control of their content.

## What I took from it

For a craftsman, the site is worth what the photographs are worth. The most
useful part of the project wasn't code: it was getting better images.
