## The need

Odoo Community covers sales, purchasing, stock and invoicing. But Helpdesk,
Appointments, Subscriptions and Rental belong to the Enterprise edition, billed
per user. For a small business, the gap between what it needs and what it can
license sits exactly there.

## What I did

A self-hosted Odoo 19 instance, installed from source, and the modules that close
that gap.

`lab_helpdesk` runs the full life of a support request: automatic reference,
customer record, team assignment, configurable stages in a kanban view,
service-level rules per team and per priority with a computed deadline and a
breach alert. Access is partitioned — a technician only sees their own teams, a
customer only their own tickets. Cross-reporting, spreadsheet export, PDF
documents.

The Sales and CRM extensions go **exclusively through Odoo's inheritance
mechanisms**. No core file is modified, which keeps the upgrade path to future
versions intact.

## What I took from it

In an ERP, the interesting constraint isn't adding a feature: it's adding one
without closing the door on the next version. Working through inheritance rather
than modification is a discipline you pay for at the first `git pull` of the
following release.
