## The need

A 200-person logistics company ran its internal IT support over email and a
shared Excel file: lost requests, no audit trail, no statistics. What was needed
was the application that replaces the spreadsheet.

## What I did

A four-developer team project over five days, starting from an in-house framework
skeleton modelled on Odoo's architecture.

Fourteen tables modelled in SQLAlchemy, with their one-to-many, one-to-one and
many-to-many relations. For every entity, **the whole stack**: model → form →
DTO → mapper → service → controller → view. JWT authentication, argon2 hashing,
many-to-many roles. Three partitioned profiles — employee, technician, IT manager
— with the ticket life cycle and its reopening path.

## What I took from it

A layered architecture is expensive on day one and pays for itself on day three,
when four people change the same domain without colliding. The direction of
dependencies isn't a classroom rule: it's what makes parallel work possible.
