## The need

A developer portfolio that doesn't look like a template. No imported component
library, no purchased theme: a visual language held by a written charter, and
code that applies it.

## What I did

All of it, from a blank page. A system of colour, rhythm and motion tokens;
hand-written primitives — a polymorphic button, an accessible field, a modal
built on `<dialog>`; an SVG map background with its own grain and relief
filters. Content lives in MDX, compiled at build time by Velite and read through
a tested selector layer.

The contact form runs on a Server Action, works **without JavaScript**, and folds
itself into a sealed envelope once the dispatch is sent.

Two languages, routed by segment, with `hreflang` and paired translations.

## What I took from it

Writing your own charter before coding changes the order of the questions. You no
longer pick a colour: you check that a contrast ratio holds, and you know why the
rule exists. Decisions get argued in a document, not in a code review.
