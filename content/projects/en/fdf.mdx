## The need

Read a two-dimensional height map and draw it as a three-dimensional wireframe
relief. No graphics engine: a library that opens a window and lights pixels,
nothing more.

## What I did

Isometric projection, segment drawing through Bresenham's algorithm, the
transformations — rotation, zoom, translation — and the keyboard and mouse event
loop. All written from the mathematics, not from an API.

## What I took from it

Drawing a straight line is a problem solved in 1962, and rediscovering it
yourself teaches more than a dozen tutorials. That's where geometry stops being a
school subject and becomes a tool.
