## The need

Rebuild the mechanics of a shell: command-line parsing, pipes, redirections,
environment variables, process and signal handling. All by hand, with no
dependency.

## What I did

A parser that splits, quotes and expands the typed line; execution of built-in
and external commands; pipes and redirections; exit-code and signal handling.
Particular attention went to matching `bash`'s real behaviour, and to leaving no
memory leaks.

## What I took from it

You think you know a shell until you have to decide exactly what `echo "a"'b'c`
does — and find the answer lives in a state machine. Low-level projects don't
teach a language, they teach you to read a specification all the way through.
